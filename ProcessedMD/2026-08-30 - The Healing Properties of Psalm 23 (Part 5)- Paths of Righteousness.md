## Summary
Before we get to the way we walk and the paths we take, we must first make doubly sure that we are following - not leading. His sheep hear, know and follow their shepherds voice, and another they will not follow. Not themselves or another person.  And the paths of God are always, ALWAYS righteous paths.  Paths that require the denial of self, and a surrender to Lordship.  Paths that are first walked by the Lord and us as we follow him.Divine paths, specific paths, narrow paths and paths that resonate with the Spirit of God within us.
